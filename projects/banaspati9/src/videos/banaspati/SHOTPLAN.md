## Grammar (the fix — binding for every shot)

1. **Geography first.** Every location change opens with an ESTABLISHING
   WIDE showing the place and its layout (house — road — bridge over the
   kali — far bamboo forest). The viewer must always be able to answer:
   *"di sini ada apa saja?"*
2. **Environment share.** In most shots the surroundings occupy a real,
   readable share of the frame — bridge, kali, trees, house, gentong are
   IN the picture, lit well enough to name. No flat wall behind a centred
   character.
3. **Group staging in space.** Scenes with several people keep the others
   visibly present and readable in frame (full bodies in shared space),
   never cropped/blurred away by a face-zoom on the speaker.
4. **Narration-acting in space.** Medium/wide shots where the described
   action visibly happens (throwing, running, leaping, filling the
   gentong) — not faces talking.
5. **Scale discipline.** Never the same scale twice in a row. Close-ups
   rationed to reaction beats only: exactly ONE in the episode (L17
   whisper). Scale budget ≈ W 30% / MW 45% / M 25% / CU 2%.
   **Measurable class bars (widened 28 Nov — see the amendment at the
   bottom): W = main figure ≤ ±1/3 of frame height with environment
   visible on all sides; MW = main figure ≤ ±1/2 of frame height,
   environment still readable. The full render inherits these bars,
   not the looser v2 framings.**
6. **No zooming.** Cameras HOLD, PAN or TRACK laterally, or CUT. A slow
   push-in is reserved for exactly TWO beats: the fire growing (L16–17)
   and the reveal (L18–20). DOF stays shallow-blur-free (cInf 3–5) so the
   surroundings stay sharp and readable.
7. **Named = shown.** When a line names a place or object (air/kali,
   jembatan, batu, senter, rumput, gentong), that thing is in the frame,
   readable — never implied off-screen.
8. **Climax rule.** The reveal gets ONE full readable WIDE of the inverted
   fire man standing on the bridge — whole figure in frame, bridge + kali
   visible — BEFORE any closer shot.
9. **Light.** Night = moonlit-clear, phone-readable: faces, hands, sets,
   ground all readable in every shot; darkness only as mood at frame
   edges; the fire is the brightest accent by contrast, not by everything
   else being black. Dawn/dusk codas brighter still.

Scales: **W** wide/establishing (figure ≤ ±1/3 frame height) · **MW**
medium-wide (figure ≤ ±1/2 frame height) · **M** medium · **CU**
close-up (rationed). Bars as amended 28 Nov 2026 (below).

**Reveal note (S7, the old still-6 "pilar api" flag).** Two widened
variants were rendered: front (kept as `still-6-alt-front.jpg`) and
profile. The FRONT still reads as a fire pillar frozen — two merged

as one diagonal mass, the arms hang as two separate columns, and the

limb gaps stay dark. Expect the unfold + hand-walking gait in motion to
finish the read; if the user rejects the frozen silhouette outright,
the fix is a figure redraw (out of scope for a camera pass).
